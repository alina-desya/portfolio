errors:
  - code: PAGE_NOT_FOUND
    solution: Check the address for typos. Everything on this site lives under a short, lowercase path.
  - code: LINK_OUTDATED
    solution: The page may have moved during a restructure. Try one of the pages below.
  - code: DOCUMENTATION_DEBT
    solution: Still stuck? Every broken link is a small piece of documentation debt, and I'd like to fix it.
    action: report
