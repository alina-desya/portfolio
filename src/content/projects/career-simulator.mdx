## About the project

Most AI career tools start after you've already chosen a target role: rewriting your CV, drafting cover letters, preparing for interviews. The Career Transition Simulator starts earlier.

It's a structured, AI-assisted conversation that helps you see how your experience reads in the market, compare realistic directions, and identify what to test before making a major change. Built as a proof-of-concept for Grace Hopper Celebration 2026, it demonstrates the Reverse Prompting Framework: a structured approach to using AI as a thinking partner rather than an answer machine.


## What I built

A four-stage prompt framework (AI skill) that structures the career exploration conversation:

- Market Lens Analysis — how a target role is positioned in current market demand, or what directions are plausible when no target exists yet
- Hiring Simulation — AI reviews your background from a recruiter's perspective and surfaces blind spots and likely objections
- Gap Calibration — distinguishes competency gaps, evidence gaps, positioning gaps, and exposure gaps — not just "what's missing"
- Transition Strategy — turns findings into no more than three time-bounded next actions

The framework is open source and designed to work across any country, industry, or career stage.
